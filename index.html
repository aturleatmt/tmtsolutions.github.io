Business Central explained.

What is Business Central?

Business Central is Microsoft ERP software that supports core financial and operational processes.

Which businesses is Business Central for?

Business Central is designed especially for small and midsize businesses.

Can Business Central be customized?

Yes. In addition to standard features, extensions and integrations are available.

How is Business Central deployed?

Depending on your requirements, Business Central can run in the Microsoft Cloud or on premises.
