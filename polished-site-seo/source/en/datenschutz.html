Contact requests and support

According to the form implementation, messages are sent by email to info@tmtsolutions.de. The form itself does not create a support ticket; support requests may also be submitted through the linked external helpdesk. The recipients and data processing of that helpdesk must be checked separately.

Attachments include their file names and contents. The implementation allows up to three files and 5 MB in total, in PDF, Office, CSV and PNG/JPG formats. Please send only material needed for a Business Central/ERP inquiry, support request or job application.

TMT Solutions must determine and obtain legal review of the applicable legal basis and retention period for inquiries and job applications.
